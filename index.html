DATA ANALYSIS: The Most Successful Horror Movie Sequels, Prequels, Remakes & Spin-offs

An analysis of 400+ horror movies and 80 horror franchises, where 1 point was given to the sequel, prequel, remake or spin-off which had a higher performance than the first movie in the given horror franchise in each of the following categories: worldwide gross, estimated return on investment, Metacritic score, IMDb rating, and Rotten Tomatoes score.
